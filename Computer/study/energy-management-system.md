Merits of Fuel Cell Vehicles (FCVs)


1. High Efficiency – Fuel cells convert hydrogen to electricity more efficiently than internal combustion engines.
2. Zero Tailpipe Emissions – Only water vapor is produced, making FCVs environmentally clean.
3. Fast Refuelling – Hydrogen tanks can be refilled within 3–5 minutes, similar to petrol vehicles.
4. Long Driving Range – FCVs generally offer longer range compared to battery electric vehicles (BEVs).
5. Quiet Operation – Fuel cell systems produce very low noise and vibration.
6. Lightweight Energy Storage – Hydrogen storage offers higher energy density, useful for heavy vehicles (buses, trucks)
7. Reduced Dependency on Fossil Fuels – Hydrogen can be produced from renewable sources.
---
Demerits of Fuel Cell Vehicles (FCVs)

1. High Cost – Fuel cells and hydrogen storage tanks are expensive due to advanced materials and technology.
2. Limited Refuelling Infrastructure – Very few hydrogen refuelling stations exist, making FCV use difficult.
3. Hydrogen Production Challenges – Most hydrogen is produced from natural gas, which is not fully clean.
4. Storage and Safety Issues – Hydrogen is highly flammable and requires high-pressure tanks.
5. Durability Issues – Fuel cells have shorter life spans compared to batteries or engines.
6. Energy Losses in Production – Producing, compressing, and transporting hydrogen consumes significant energy.
7. Maintenance Complexity – Fuel cell systems are technically complex, increasing maintenance difficulty.
